Analyst Program: Students who complete the Investor Training Program and are selected for the Analyst Program go through analyst training upon joining. Analysts are then placed on a pitch team, where they contribute to the entire investment process, from idea generation to ongoing coverage of portfolio positions. Throughout the program, analysts get mentorship from senior members and full access to club resources.

General Training Schedule

Lesson 1: Introduction to Markets and the Economy
Lesson 2: Equities, Fixed Income, and Other Asset Classes
Lesson 3: Principles of Investing
Lesson 4: Reading Financial Statements
Lesson 5: Valuation
Lesson 6: Discounted Cash Flow Analysis
Lesson 7: Building a Stock Pitch
Office Hours and Pitch Prep
Final Stock Pitches

More details will be shared with participants during the first lessons. Please reach out to the executive board with any questions about the program.
