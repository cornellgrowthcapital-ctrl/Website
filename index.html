About Us

Cornell Growth Capital is a student-run investment organization at Cornell University. Founded in 2026, the club brings together students from every school and major who want to understand how markets work. Our purpose is two-fold: to develop disciplined, research-driven investors, and to build a community of students who share a passion for investing.
